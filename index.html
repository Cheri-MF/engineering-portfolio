01 / PROFILE

Process development,
grounded in evidence.

My work spans laboratory investigations, early-stage process engineering and technical evaluation. At Free Radical Process Design, I supported hydrometallurgical, electrochemical and industrial water-treatment projects through experimental programmes, process calculations, flow diagrams and technical reporting.

02 / SELECTED WORK

Selected engineering work.

A closer look at my contributions, with laboratory work, research and proposed applications clearly distinguished.

01 / PROCESS DEVELOPMENT

PROJECT 01 · MINERAL PROCESSING

Manganese recovery & processing

Contributed to design-basis and process documentation for a manganese sulphate monohydrate demonstration plant. Planned and ran selective-precipitation investigations for manganese fines, with preliminary electrolysis screening.

SELECTIVE PRECIPITATION · BENEFICIATION

PROJECT 02 · ELECTROCHEMISTRY

Zinc electrolysis trials

Planned, set up, ran and reported plate-electrode zinc electrolysis trials end to end, including experimental design, sampling, analysis and recommendations.

ZINC ELECTROLYSIS · EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN

PROJECT 03 · REACTOR DEVELOPMENT

Continuous rotating-cathode electrowinning

Ran hands-on copper and cobalt trials on a continuous rotating-cathode electrowinning unit, studying deposit form, scraping and release.

Scope of the zinc proposal

Contributed to a proposal to apply the concept to zinc recovery from tailings water, including scale-up considerations; this zinc application was proposed rather than demonstrated.

ELECTROWINNING · SCALE-UP CONSIDERATIONS

PROJECT 04 · INDUSTRIAL WASTEWATER

Mine-tailings water treatment

Investigated metal removal from synthetic mine-tailings water using oxidation and selective precipitation.

Research proposal contribution

Wrote the laboratory-trial section of a research proposal for iron, zinc and manganese recovery from Peruvian tailings water.

OXIDATION · SELECTIVE PRECIPITATION · RESEARCH PROPOSAL

PROJECT 05 · REACTOR TESTING

Gold recovery from refinery eluate

Ran laboratory electrowinning/sludging reactor trials on refinery eluate, varying current density, voltage, flow rate and residence time. Supported mass-balance calculations and ICP-OES/XRF data analysis.

ELECTROWINNING · REACTOR TESTING · DATA ANALYSIS

PROJECT 06 · PUBLISHED RESEARCH

Phlogopite leaching kinetics

Designed nitric-acid leaching experiments and characterised mineral feed and residues. Identified diffusion-controlled kinetics using a one-dimensional D1 model and determined activation energies. Published in Clays and Clay Minerals (2022).

LEACHING · D1 KINETIC MODEL · ACTIVATION ENERGIES

PROJECT 07 · RARE EARTHS

Rare-earth precipitation & plant economics

Ran lanthanide precipitation experiments and contributed calculations to a CAPEX/OPEX model for a neodymium metal plant.

LANTHANIDE PRECIPITATION · CAPEX/OPEX

PROJECT 08 · ELECTROCHEMISTRY

Continuous cobalt electrowinning

Conducted experimental investigations for cobalt electrowinning, supporting evaluation of operating conditions and continuous-process development.

ELECTROWINNING · PROCESS DEVELOPMENT

Other projects

Other work included Fe/Al precipitation investigations, a Ni-MH battery-recycling literature review, techno-economic evaluation of neodymium metal production, a galvanising-process review and a seawater electrolysis literature review.

My academic work also included a portable membrane-based water purification concept. On Research Vessel Rosa, I supported vessel operations and gained practical exposure to pumps and reverse-osmosis watermakers.
